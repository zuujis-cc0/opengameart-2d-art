Reactorcore Art (RC Art)
==
PACK: Alphabet Bombs

--

Pixel-art sprites of small round objects with a letter of the alphabet on them. Originally inspired by Star Fox's Smart Bomb. 

PNG images with a transparent background. Also a sheet with all bombs included on it is available too.

--

The idea is to come up with a cool idea for a bomb based on the letter of the alphabet. First think of a word that starts with a particular letter and then create an idea how the bomb works and what it does.

Example:

F - Flame, Fire, Flamethrower, Field, Force, Flash

"Flamethrower Bomb" - Upon detonation releases a device with nozzles that shoots a jet of fire in 6 directions for 2 straight minutes. Can roll on flat surfaces, while the jets actually push and spin the device around through sheer force.

The bomb graphic itself serves as a shell for this "surprise". See how many fun ideas you can come up with - put that dictionary to good use!

--

By default in image import settings, all sprites/textures have been set to 32 pixels per unit and using a point filter so they appear crisp.
