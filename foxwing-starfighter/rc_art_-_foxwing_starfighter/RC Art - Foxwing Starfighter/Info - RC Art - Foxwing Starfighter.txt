Reactorcore Art (RC Art)
==
PACK: Foxwing Starfighter

--

Pixel-art sprites pack depicting a top-down view of a high speed starfighter for space and aerial combat missions.

Includes a blue, red and green versions. The seperate wings are intended to be freely attached to the body sprite and rotated with pivots.

--

By default in image import settings, all sprites/textures have been set to 32 pixels per unit and using a point filter so they appear crisp.
