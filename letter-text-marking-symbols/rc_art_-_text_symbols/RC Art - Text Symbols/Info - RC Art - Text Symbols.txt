Reactorcore Art (RC Art)
==
PACK: Text Symbols

--

Pixel-art tile sprites of every symbol in the alphabet from A to Z and numbers 0 to 9. Intended for signs and markings inside a tech facility.

PNG images with a transparent background. Both individual images and full sheets included. 

--

Sheets are 12x12 pixels, with 36 symbols in total.

--

By default in image import settings, all sprites/textures have been set to 32 pixels per unit and using a point filter so they appear crisp.
